About

A locals' pub, tucked away close to the centre.

The Crossways sits a short walk from King's Lynn station, on the corner of South Everard Street. It is what a pub should be — a small room, a bar, a fireplace, real ale on the pump, and people who already know each other's order.

The pub was re-opened in June 2024 under new management, with the slow, patient work of refurbishing it under way. The bones are old and unchanged. Sit at the bar, walk through to the back for darts and pool, or step outside to the patio when the weather decides.

It is not the loudest pub in King's Lynn. That is the point.
